The TDP Model is built on one idea: time dictates price. Instead of stacking indicators, it comes down to reading when the market is likely to move — then confirming it with a handful of clear signals, not guesswork.

It's the same process taught throughout The Trader Academy's curriculum — spotting manipulation and displacement, stacking confluences, calling reversals, and riding continuations once a move is already underway.

Taught in full, the same way it's actually traded — nothing held back for a later upsell.

The TDP Model

How it works

Time Dictates Price

Price only moves once time gives its signal — once you can see the timing, direction stops being a guess.

Confluences

Multiple signals lining up before an entry — corroborating evidence stacked together, not a single indicator.

Manipulation & Reversals

Spotting the liquidity grabs that shake out early entries, and reading when that move is about to turn.

Continuations

Recognizing when a move already in motion still has room left to run — and riding it instead of exiting early.
